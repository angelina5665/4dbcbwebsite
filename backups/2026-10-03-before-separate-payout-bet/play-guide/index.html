HOW TO BET

Understand a basic 4D entry in five steps

  1. 1
    Choose a provider

    Start with the operator and product named on the play slip.

  2. 2
    Enter four digits

    Use exactly four digits from 0000 to 9999, including leading zeroes.

  3. 3
    Choose straight or permutation

    Straight keeps one order. IBOX/i-Perm covers the number’s unique arrangements under provider rules.

  4. 4
    Choose Big or Small

    Big includes top-three, special/starter and consolation categories. Small covers only the top three but has higher published prizes.

  5. 5
    Review before confirming

    Check the number, provider, draw, play type, pool and amount on the real ticket.
