Source, permission and coverage

Available records: Magnum 25 April 1985–20 September 2026; Sports Toto 6 May 1992–20 September 2026; Da Ma Cai 1 January 2005–20 September 2026. Sabah 88, Sandakan STC and Cash Sweep currently have only 5–6 September 2026. Sports Toto's older source records omit many consolation prizes. Missing results are not proof that a number never won.

Historical Magnum, Sports Toto and Da Ma Cai data: malaysia-4d public dataset (MIT), derived from operator sources. Regional records: retained 4DVIP88 files compared with 4d4d.co on 8 September 2026. These checks are not independent operator certification. Read the method.

Republishing permission: site owner reports that leadership obtained approval for result information. The source dataset states an MIT licence; no operator endorsement is claimed. Verify important information with the operator.
