Malaysia 4D Prize Guide

A factual guide to common labels used in 4D result tables.

What the common result labels mean

A result table identifies numbers by category; it does not by itself determine what a ticket is worth. Read the provider, product, draw date and draw number before comparing any number.

Common labels used on the result pages
LabelWhat this site displaysWhat to check
First, second and third prizeThe three top result-number positions for the named draw.Match the exact number, including any leading zero, and keep it with its displayed position.
SpecialA separate group of published result numbers.Do not treat a special number as a top-three result.
ConsolationAnother separately labelled group of result numbers.Keep the category label when recording or comparing the result.
3D, 5D, 6D or lottoProduct-specific formats shown only when present in the retained source snapshot.Use that product's own labels and rules; these fields are not ordinary 4D categories.

Why fixed prize amounts are not listed here

Prize value, eligibility and claim rules can depend on the provider, product, ticket type, stake, draw and current terms. Those rules can change independently of the result number. 4DVIP88 therefore does not infer a payout from a number, promise that a ticket wins, or present an imported jackpot figure as claim advice.

For a monetary value or claim decision, use the relevant provider's current rules and the information printed on the ticket. The result pages are an independent factual reference, not a substitute for provider confirmation.

A careful checking sequence

  1. Choose the provider and product named on the ticket or record.
  2. Match the provider draw date and draw number.
  3. Compare every digit exactly and preserve leading zeroes.
  4. Keep the number with its first, second, third, special or consolation label.
  5. Verify prize value, eligibility and claim details with the relevant provider.
